How replaceable are you?

Answer 5 questions in your own words. An AI reads them and gives you a brutally honest score.

How the score works

The quiz looks at five things: what your job is, what you actually do all day, which parts need human judgment or a physical presence, how repetitive your week is, and whether you already use AI tools. An AI reads your answers and estimates how much of your work could be automated. A higher percentage means more of your tasks are routine, screen-based and rule-driven. A lower percentage means your work depends on people, trust, creativity or hands-on skill.

Is the score accurate?

It's an estimate for fun, not a prediction. Real jobs are mixes of tasks, and AI usually changes jobs before it replaces them.

Which jobs score lowest?

Work built on in-person care, skilled trades, leadership and original creative direction tends to score lower. Repetitive data, paperwork and basic writing tasks tend to score higher.

How can I lower my score?

Get great at using AI tools in your field, build skills that need people and trust, and aim to own decisions rather than only carry out tasks.
