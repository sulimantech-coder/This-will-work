How replaceable are you?

About

This is a 60-second quiz that asks five questions about your work and uses AI to estimate how much of it could be automated in the coming years.

How it works

You type short answers in your own words. They're sent to an AI model, which returns a score from 1 to 99 (higher means easier to automate), a short label, a two-sentence explanation and three tips. If the AI is busy, a simple built-in estimate is used instead.

What it isn't

Entertainment, not career advice. No quiz can predict what will happen to a real job, and every job is a mix of tasks.

Your privacy

We don't ask for your name or create accounts. Please don't type personal or sensitive information. See the privacy policy.

Why we built it

Everyone is talking about AI and jobs, but most of it is panic or hype. We wanted something quick, honest and shareable that gets people thinking about which human skills matter most.
